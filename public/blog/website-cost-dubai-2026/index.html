How Much Does a Website Cost in Dubai in 2026?

By Atlantic Bear Team  ·  Published August 2026  ·  7 min read

If you run a small business in Dubai and you've started looking into getting a website built, you've probably already noticed that prices vary wildly. One freelancer quotes AED 500. An agency comes back with AED 25,000. An AI website builder says you can do it yourself for free. It's confusing, and it's easy to either overpay massively or end up with something that doesn't actually help your business.

This article breaks down every option clearly, with real price ranges for the UAE market in 2026, so you can make an informed decision.

The Main Options (and What They Actually Cost)

Option 1: DIY Website Builders

Platforms like Wix, Squarespace, and WordPress.com let you build your own site using drag-and-drop editors. The subscription itself is cheap, usually AED 300–900 per year. But the real cost isn't the subscription. It's your time.

A proper business website requires copywriting, design decisions, mobile optimization, SEO setup, and ongoing updates. If you're running a business, spending 40–60 hours on a website you'll end up being unhappy with isn't really "free."

DIY also has real limitations: templates look generic, you're locked into the platform, and the SEO capabilities are limited. If a competitor has a professionally designed custom site, yours will look like a hobby blog in comparison.

Best for: Hobby projects, personal blogs, or very early-stage startups testing a concept on zero budget.

Option 2: Hiring a Freelancer in Dubai or the UAE

The freelancer market in the UAE varies enormously. Entry-level freelancers charge AED 1,500–3,000 for a basic website. Experienced ones with strong portfolios can charge AED 5,000–15,000+.

The challenge with freelancers is reliability and ongoing support. A freelancer who builds your site may not be available six months later when you need a content update. Many small business owners have had the experience of a freelancer going quiet after delivery, leaving them unable to change anything on their own site.

You also need to manage the project yourself (hosting, domain, email, updates), which adds hidden costs and time.

Best for: Businesses with a technical point of contact who can manage the relationship and handle small issues independently.

Option 3: Large Digital Agencies in Dubai

Dubai has dozens of large full-service agencies. Their websites feature impressive case studies, large teams, and slick presentations. Prices typically start at AED 15,000–25,000 for a basic corporate website and can go well over AED 100,000 for complex builds.

The quality is generally high. But the cost and timeline are prohibitive for most small businesses. A 3-month timeline means you're waiting almost a quarter of the year before your website is even live. And once delivered, ongoing support is expensive.

Best for: Large corporations, government entities, or businesses with significant budgets and complex requirements.

What's Actually Included in a "Good" Website?

When comparing prices, make sure you're comparing like-for-like. A complete small business website should include:

  • Custom design (not a template someone else is using)
  • Professional copywriting (not placeholder "Lorem ipsum" text)
  • Mobile-responsive layout (essential in the UAE where 80%+ of browsing is mobile)
  • SSL certificate (the padlock icon, required for Google trust)
  • Basic SEO setup: Google Search Console, sitemap, page titles
  • Fast loading speed (under 3 seconds on mobile)
  • WhatsApp integration (critical in the UAE market)
  • Hosting and domain (often hidden extras)

Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

A headline price of AED 1,500 can quickly become AED 4,000+ when you add:

  • Domain registration: AED 50–200/year
  • Hosting: AED 300–1,200/year (varies wildly)
  • SSL certificate: Free–AED 500/year
  • Revision rounds: Some agencies charge AED 200–500 per change request
  • Maintenance: AED 500–2,000/year for basic upkeep
